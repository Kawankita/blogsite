## Brand & Style

This design system drives a hybrid editorial news portal and publication blog, fusing high-velocity breaking journalism with long-form analytical reading. The aesthetic leans into a modern, refined technical minimalism tailored for dark environments. It prioritizes legibility, typographic rhythm, and deep spatial immersion.

The visual style eliminates visual clutter in favor of high-contrast editorial hierarchy, precise surface layering, and controlled luminescent purple focal points. Interactions feel direct, responsive, and tactile without drifting into gaudy skeuomorphism or distracting glassmorphic filters.

## Colors

The palette establishes visual structure across deep navy-tinted charcoal backgrounds:

- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `Surface Canvas`: `#0C111D` (root screen backdrop).
  - `Surface Container`: `#161B26` (article listing cards, secondary widgets, sidebars).
  - `Surface High`: `#1F242F` (hero editorial cards, spotlight features, newsletter CTA containers).
- **Brand & Accent:**
  - `Primary / Accent`: `#9E77ED` (interactive highlights, category badges, active links, primary CTA fill).
  - `Primary Hover`: `#7F56D9` (interactive hover states).
  - `Accent Tint`: `rgba(158, 119, 237, 0.15)` (pill background tints, subtle highlight states).
- **Typography:**
  - `Text Primary`: `#F9FAFB` (headlines, display text, lead intros).
  - `Text Secondary`: `#CECFD2` (article body copy, synopsis excerpts).
  - `Text Tertiary`: `#94969C` (byline meta, timestamps, reading durations, kicker tags).
  - `Primary Button Text`: `#0C111D` (ensures AA/AAA contrast over the `#9E77ED` primary button fill).
- **Dividers & Structure:**
  - `Border Subtle`: `#222938` (structural dividers, card perimeters).
  - `Border Strong`: `#333B48` (form outlines, active focus borders, high-priority frames).

## Typography

The typography pairs Plus Jakarta Sans for structural personality with Inter for utilitarian reading clarity:

- **Display & Headlines:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers a confident, geometric editorial presence with tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to avoid looseness in dark interfaces.
- **Editorial Body:** Inter provides high x-height and neutral geometry designed to prevent reader fatigue across analytical prose, set at comfortable reading line-height ratios (1.55 to 1.6).
- **Metadata & Badges:** Uppercase and semi-bold sub-headings take slightly expanded tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) to maintain legibility when displayed in tertiary gray `#94969C` against `#161B26` containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column responsive fluid grid with fixed max-width constraints for editorial reading optimization:

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Desktop: `1280px` max content container; 12 columns; `gutter`: 24px (`1.5rem`); outer margin: 32px (`2rem`).
  - Tablet: `768px` to `1024px`; 8 columns; `gutter`: 16px (`1rem`); outer margin: 24px (`1.5rem`).
  - Mobile: `< 768px`; 4 columns; `gutter-mobile`: 16px (`1rem`); `margin-mobile`: 16px (`1rem`).
- **Reading Columns:** Editorial article bodies collapse into a dedicated center-aligned 720px column to preserve optimal characters-per-line (65–75 characters) regardless of viewport width.
- **Grid Density:** News streams use compact card gaps (`space-md` or 16px), whereas long-form featured layouts expand to `space-xl` (32px) to denote analytical priority.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tonal separation and low-contrast borders rather than heavy, drop-shadow blurs. In this deep dark environment, standard black shadows disappear; thus, edges and surfaces establish spatial hierarchy:

1. **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#0C111D` (base viewport).
2. **Level 1 (Card Standard):** `#161B26` enclosed by a 1px solid `#222938` boundary. Hovering shifts the border to `#333B48` with a subtle luminous tint (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(158, 119, 237, 0.08)`).
3. **Level 2 (Hero & Elevated Units):** `#1F242F` container with a 1px border of `#333B48`.
4. **Level 3 (Dropdowns, Overlays & Sticky Nav):** `#161B26` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, an outline border of `#333B48`, and directional edge shadow `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a tiered geometric rounding model aligned with functional hierarchy:

- **Small Components (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to buttons, form inputs, badge pills, utility chips, and interactive dropdown triggers.
- **Medium Containers (12px / `0.75rem`):** Applied to news feed cards, thumbnail wrappers, media embeds, and comment boxes.
- **Large Banners & Frames (24px / `1.5rem`):** Reserved for hero story canvases, major promotional containers, breaking news lead banners, and newsletter subscription cards.
- **Full Radius (9999px):** Status badges, live broadcast indicators, and avatar portraits.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Background `#9E77ED`, text `#0C111D`, border radius 8px, font weight 600. On hover: background transitions to `#7F56D9`. On active: subtle scale shift (0.98).
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Background transparent, border 1px solid `#333B48`, text `#F9FAFB`. On hover: background `#1F242F`, border `#9E77ED`.
- **Chips & Kicker Tags:**
  - Border radius 8px (or full pill for status indicators). Background `rgba(158, 119, 237, 0.15)`, text `#9E77ED`, font size 12px, font weight 600, uppercase letter-spacing.
  - Live/Breaking chip: Background `rgba(240, 68, 56, 0.15)`, text `#F04438`, accompanied by an animated pulsing dot.
- **Cards (Editorial & Feed):**
  - *Feed Card:* Background `#161B26`, border 1px solid `#222938`, border radius 12px, inner padding `space-lg`. Image corners clipped at 8px radius.
  - *Hero Card:* Background `#1F242F`, border 1px solid `#333B48`, border radius 24px, inner padding `space-xl`.
- **Form Inputs & Search:**
  - Background `#161B26`, border 1px solid `#222938`, border radius 8px, text `#F9FAFB`, placeholder `#94969C`. On focus: border 1px solid `#9E77ED`, ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(158, 119, 237, 0.20)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Base square/circle in `#161B26` with 1px solid `#333B48`. When checked: fill `#9E77ED`, icon `#0C111D`.
- **Editorial Meta Lists & Dividers:**
  - Inline metadata displays inline dot separators in `#333B48`. Horizontal rules across article bodies render as 1px solid `#222938`.
- **Breaking News Ticker:**
  - Fixed-height horizontal banner positioned below navigation. Background `#161B26`, border-bottom 1px solid `#222938`, lead badge in primary purple or alert red, followed by ticker text in `#CECFD2`.
